# Dashboard Overview

The Renova dashboard is your restaurant's control center. Everything from your menu to your orders to your staff is managed here.

## Key Sections

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Menu Editor** | Build and manage your digital menu |
| **Orders** | View incoming and historical orders |
| **Bookings** | Manage table reservations |
| **QR Codes** | Generate and download table QR codes |
| **Feedback** | Read customer reviews and ratings |
| **Staff** | Invite team members and assign roles |
| **Settings** | Configure restaurant details, hours, and payments |
| **Billing** | Manage your Renova subscription |
