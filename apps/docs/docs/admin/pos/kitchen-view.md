# Kitchen Display

The kitchen display at `/staff/kitchen` is designed for a shared kitchen screen or tablet. Kitchen-role devices are directed to this view after authentication.

Active orders are grouped into **Awaiting Payment**, **New**, **In Progress**, and **Ready** columns. Paid or pay-later orders enter the preparation flow; advancing an order moves it through **New → In Progress → Served → Completed**. Cards include table numbers, ordered items, special requests, and elapsed timers.

Completed orders leave the active board and remain available in the built-in history for 24 hours. Failed status updates stay visible so staff can retry instead of losing the action.
