# Subscriptions & Billing

Renova provides four subscription plans designed to support restaurants at every stage—from a newly opened café looking for a clean QR code menu to a bustling multi-room venue operating full kitchen displays, tableside POS devices, and thermal printers.

---

## Subscription Plans & Feature Comparison

### 1. Free (€0 forever)
Designed for venues that want to replace paper menus with a fast, modern QR code experience.
- **Includes**: Digital Menu Editor, QR code generation and printing, menu import and export (JSON, CSV, XLSX), and basic scan tracking (total views and scans per table).
- **Staff Seats**: 0 additional staff accounts (managed by the owner).

### 2. Starter (€15 / month)
Designed for small venues that want guests to order from their tables and call staff.
- **Includes**: Everything in Free, plus online table ordering, guest carts, call waiter / table assistance, multi-language auto-translation, and daily order summaries.
- **Staff Seats**: 1 additional staff seat.

### 3. Professional (€25 / month) — *Recommended*
Designed for active restaurants and bars adding digital payments, loyalty rewards, and bookings.
- **Includes**: Everything in Starter, plus pay-at-table (card and digital payment gateways), full sales analytics and Excel export, custom brand colors and fonts, loyalty program with VIP tiers and happy hours, customer accounts, smart upselling, menu scheduling (dayparting), table reservations, and manager roles.
- **Staff Seats**: Up to 5 staff seats.

### 4. Enterprise (€45 / month)
Designed for full-service restaurants, hotels, and high-volume operations running frontline hardware and specialized roles.
- **Includes**: Everything in Professional, plus the tableside Waiter POS, Kitchen Display System (KDS), fast 4-digit PIN login for waitstaff and kitchen crew, table zones and sections, thermal receipt printer integration, and custom receipt branding.
- **Staff Seats**: Unlimited staff accounts.

:::

## Who Can Manage Subscriptions

Only the **Restaurant Owner** can view billing information, upgrade or downgrade plans, and access invoices. Managers and staff members cannot access the Subscription tab.

---

## How to Upgrade Your Plan

1. In your dashboard sidebar, click **Settings**, then select the **Subscription** tab (or click **Pricing** in the main navigation).
2. Review the plan comparison and choose your preferred billing cycle (**Monthly** or **Yearly**).
3. Click **Upgrade Plan** or **Choose Plan** on the tier you wish to activate.
4. You will be redirected to the secure checkout page.
5. Enter your payment details and click **Subscribe**.
6. Once payment is confirmed, your new features and tools unlock immediately in your dashboard.

---

## Managing Invoices and Payment Methods

1. Go to **Settings > Subscription**.
2. Click **Manage Billing (Stripe Portal)**.
3. In the self-service portal, you can:
   - Update your credit or debit card on file.
   - View your upcoming billing date and amount.
   - Download official PDF receipts and invoices for your accounting team.
   - Cancel or switch your subscription.
4. When finished, click the return link to go back to your Renova dashboard.

---

## Locked Features and Upgrade Prompts

If your current plan does not include a particular tool (for example, attempting to open full revenue analytics on the Free plan, or opening the Waiter POS on the Professional plan), an upgrade modal will appear explaining which tier is required. Clicking **Upgrade** from this modal will take you directly to the plan selection screen with your current settings preserved.

---

## Important Notes

- **Prorated Upgrades**: When you upgrade mid-cycle, you are only billed the prorated difference for the remainder of that billing month or year.
- **Downgrading**: If you choose to downgrade your plan, your higher-tier features remain active until the end of your prepaid billing period, after which the plan will adjust automatically.
- **Separate Venue Subscriptions**: If you manage multiple restaurant locations, each venue can be subscribed to the plan that matches its specific operations.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Payment Fails During Renewal**: You will receive an email notification with a secure link to update your card. You can also open **Settings > Subscription > Manage Billing (Stripe Portal)** at any time to add a new payment method.
- **Features Do Not Appear After Upgrading**: If you complete an upgrade and a new tab does not appear immediately, refresh your web browser or sign out and back in to refresh your active session.
