# Subscriptions & Billing

The platform offers a flexible 4-tier subscription model tailored to the size and needs of your venue. Billing is handled securely through Stripe.

## Subscription Tiers

You can choose from four distinct tiers. Features are progressively unlocked as you upgrade.

1. **FREE**: Ideal for trying out the platform. Includes basic QR digital menus and scan tracking, but excludes revenue-sensitive features and advanced analytics.
2. **STARTER**: Unlocks the core ordering system, allowing customers to place orders from their phones.
3. **PROFESSIONAL**: Our most popular tier. Unlocks advanced features like the Kitchen Display System (KDS), Waiter POS, full analytics, and table reservations.
4. **ENTERPRISE**: Designed for large venues and chains requiring API access, custom integrations, and dedicated support.

## Billing Cycle & Discounts

- **Monthly vs. Yearly**: You can choose to be billed monthly or annually.
- **Annual Discount**: Opting for the annual billing cycle automatically applies a 15% discount to your subscription cost.

## Upgrading & Downgrading

You can manage your subscription directly from the **Settings > Billing** view in your dashboard.
- To upgrade, click the **Upgrade Plan** button on the Pricing page. You will be redirected to a secure Stripe Checkout session. Once payment is successful, your new features are unlocked instantly.
- To change billing cycles, update payment methods, or downgrade, click the **Manage Billing (Stripe Portal)** button. This opens the Stripe Customer Portal where you have full self-service control over your subscription.

## Feature Locking

If you attempt to access a feature that is not included in your current tier (e.g., trying to view advanced analytics on the FREE plan), you will see an upgrade prompt. Navigating directly to a locked URL will securely redirect you. The platform enforces feature access heavily at the server level, ensuring data security and accurate tier gating.
