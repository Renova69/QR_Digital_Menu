# Multi-Venue Management

If you operate more than one dining room, café, bar, or regional branch, Renova allows you to manage all your locations from a single user account. You do not need to register separate login accounts for each venue.

---

## What This Feature Does

Each restaurant you create functions as an independent venue within your account. This ensures complete separation between your locations:

- **Menus**: Categories, dish items, prices, dietary tags, and schedules are customized per venue.
- **Tables & QR Codes**: Physical table layouts, zones, service points, and QR codes belong to that specific address.
- **Orders & Live Floor**: Incoming orders, active table sessions, and waiter assistance calls are routed strictly to the correct location.
- **Staff Teams**: Waiters and kitchen staff PINs are assigned to their specific restaurant.
- **Branding & Theme**: Customize logos, colors, and typography to fit each concept.
- **Analytics & History**: Track sales, scan counts, and popular items separately for each venue.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners**: Can create new venues, switch between all owned locations, manage billing, and invite managers.
- **Managers**: Can access and manage the specific venues to which they have been assigned.

---

## How to Switch Between Venues

1. Look at the top navigation bar of your dashboard.
2. Click on the restaurant name dropdown next to the Renova logo.
3. Select the venue you want to manage from the list.
4. The dashboard immediately updates to display the live orders, tables, menu, and metrics for that location.

---

## How to Add a New Restaurant Location

1. Click the restaurant dropdown at the top of your dashboard.
2. Click **Add Restaurant** (or the plus icon).
3. In the creation dialog, enter the new location's basic details:
   - **Restaurant Name**: The public trade name of the location.
   - **Phone Number & Address**: The contact information displayed on the guest menu footer.
   - **Number of Tables**: Initial table count (you can adjust this later in the Tables tab).
4. Click **Create Restaurant**.
5. Your new venue is created immediately. You can now build its menu, set its tables, and customize its branding.

---

## Setting a Custom Web Address (Vanity Slug)

You can choose a personalized web address for your restaurant's digital menu so it is easy to share on social media or printed materials:

1. Navigate to **Settings** and select the **General** tab.
2. Locate the **Vanity Slug** field.
3. Enter your preferred short name (for example, `bistro-central`).
4. Click **Save Changes**.
5. Your public menu is now accessible directly at your custom short address as well as through its table QR codes.

---

## Important Notes

- **Subscription Separation**: Subscription plans, payment processor connections, and staff seat allocations are managed on a per-restaurant basis so you can choose different plans (e.g., Free for a bar counter, Professional for a full dining room).
- **Timezone Settings**: If you manage venues in different timezones, set the local timezone individually under **Settings > General** to ensure happy hours and scheduled menus trigger at the correct local hour.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Cannot See a Venue in the Dropdown**: Ensure you are signed in with the owner email address that created the venue. If you are a manager or staff member, contact the restaurant owner to verify that your account has been added to that specific location's staff list.
- **Custom Web Address Unavailable**: If a vanity slug cannot be saved, it may already be in use by another venue. Try adding a location identifier (such as `bistro-sofia-center`).
