# Restaurant Management

The QR Digital Menu platform is built on a multi-tenant architecture, meaning a single user account can create and manage multiple distinct restaurant venues. 

## Multi-Venue Support

Whether you own a single café or a chain of restaurants, you manage them all from one centralized dashboard. 

### What is Isolated Per Restaurant?
Each restaurant you create acts as a completely independent venue. The following data and settings are isolated per restaurant:
- **Menus**: Categories, items, options, and schedules.
- **Tables & QR Codes**: The physical layout and QR routing for the venue.
- **Orders & Analytics**: Real-time sales data, order history, and customer feedback.
- **Branding & Theming**: Logos, color palettes, and fonts.
- **Loyalty Programs**: Reward tiers, points ledgers, and happy hours.
- **Staffing**: Waiters and kitchen staff assigned to the venue.

## Switching Between Venues

When you log into the dashboard, you will be prompted to select a restaurant if you manage more than one. 

You can seamlessly switch between your venues using the restaurant selector in the dashboard navigation. When you switch venues, the dashboard instantly reloads to display the real-time orders, analytics, and settings for the newly selected location.

## Settings & Configuration

For each venue, you can configure deep settings:
- **Localization**: Define the default target languages for automatic menu translation.
- **Timezone**: Set the local IANA timezone to ensure that menu dayparting (schedules), happy hours, and analytics data are perfectly accurate for that specific location, rather than defaulting to UTC server time.
- **Features**: Depending on your subscription tier, toggle specific features on or off per venue.
