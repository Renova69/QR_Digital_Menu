# Tables, Zones & QR Codes

The **Tables** workspace is where you set up your physical floor plan, organize tables into zones, generate branded QR codes for each table, and monitor live table occupancy in real time.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Table & Zone Layout**: Add tables and group them into practical zones (such as Main Dining, Terrace, Patio, or Bar) to keep your floor organized.
- **Branded QR Codes**: Renova automatically generates QR codes for every table. The QR codes incorporate your restaurant's accent color and embed your logo in the center.
- **Printable Table Tents**: Generate print-ready A4 sheets with pre-designed table tent templates (Classic, Premium, or Minimal) ready for cutting and placing on tables.
- **Live Floor Status**: A real-time visual grid showing which tables are empty, occupied, or paid and ready to be bussed.
- **Table Detail Inspection**: Click any table card to see its active order history, customer names, order totals, and session duration.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners and Managers**: Can add, edit, or delete tables, configure zones, and print QR codes.
- **Staff and Waiters**: Can view live table statuses and open table details to manage active dining sessions.

---

## How to Add Tables and Zones

1. In the dashboard sidebar, click **Tables**.
2. Select the **Tables & QR** sub-tab.
3. Click the **Add Table** button.
4. Enter the details:
   - **Table Number / Name**: Enter the identifier guests and staff use (e.g., "Table 12" or "Terrace 3").
   - **Zone**: Select an existing zone or create a new one (e.g., "Garden Patio").
5. Click **Create Table**. Renova immediately adds the table to your layout and creates its dedicated QR code.

---

## How to Download and Print QR Codes

### Option A: Download an Individual Table QR Code
1. Under **Tables > Tables & QR**, locate the table in the list.
2. Click the **QR Code** icon on that table row.
3. A modal opens displaying the branded QR code with your venue logo.
4. Click **Download PNG** to save a high-resolution image file suitable for custom graphic design or acrylic table stands.

### Option B: Bulk Print Table Tents (A4 Sheets)
1. Switch to the **Printable QR Codes** sub-tab at the top of the Tables view.
2. Choose your preferred template style:
   - **Classic**: Clean white card with elegant dashed borders.
   - **Premium**: High-contrast dark background for upscale evening venues.
   - **Minimal**: Contemporary, streamlined design.
3. Select your layout density (e.g., 2 cards per A4 page or 4 cards per page).
4. Click **Print All** to open your browser's print dialog, or click **Save as PDF** to send the file to a print shop.

## Using the Live Table View

Switch to the **Live View** sub-tab to monitor your dining room during service:

- **Gray Card (Empty)**: The table is available with no active dining session.
- **Amber Card (Occupied)**: Guests are seated and have opened a session or placed orders. The card displays the elapsed time and current order count.
- **Green Card (Paid)**: The bill has been settled digitally or closed by waitstaff. The table is ready to be cleared for the next party.

Click on any table card to open the **Table Details Modal**:
- Review all dishes ordered by this table, separated by order time.
- See whether orders came from the customer QR code or the Waiter POS.
- Review total bill amounts in dual currencies.
- Click **Close Session** when guests depart to reset the table to Empty.

---

## Managing Existing Tables

- **Edit Table Name or Zone**: Click the edit pencil icon on any table row, update its name or zone, and click **Save**.
- **Delete a Table**: Click the trash icon on the table row and confirm the prompt.
- **Filter by Zone**: In the Live View, use the zone filter pills at the top to focus exclusively on specific dining areas (e.g., viewing only the "Terrace").

---

## Important Notes

- **Unique Destinations**: Each table's QR code encodes a specific link that automatically binds the guest's phone to that exact table number. Do not copy the same QR code across multiple physical tables.
- **Paid Session Auto-Close**: When a table is paid digitally via pay-at-table, Renova turns the card green and automatically resets it to empty after 5 minutes if not closed manually.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Guests Scan and See the Wrong Table Number**: Verify that the physical QR tent on the table matches the table number printed on it.
- **QR Code Fails to Scan Under Dim Light**: Ensure printed table tents have good contrast (dark code on light paper, or use the Premium template). If using acrylic holders, check that plastic glare is minimized.
