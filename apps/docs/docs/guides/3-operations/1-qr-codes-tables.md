# QR Codes & Tables

The platform revolves around table-specific QR codes. By embedding the table number directly into the QR code, customers don't need to manually enter their location when ordering.

## Creating Tables

In the **Tables** view of your dashboard, you can define your venue's layout.
- Create tables with specific names or numbers (e.g., "Table 1", "Bar 3", "Terrace 5").
- Assign tables to specific **Zones** to help your staff navigate large venues more easily (especially within the Waiter POS).

## QR Code Generation & Printing

Once you create a table, the system automatically generates a unique QR code for it. 
- **Branded QR Codes**: The generated QR codes automatically incorporate your restaurant's accent color and embed your logo in the center. They use high error correction to ensure they scan reliably even with a logo.
- **Single Download**: You can download a high-resolution PNG of any individual QR code.
- **Bulk Printing**: The platform includes a built-in printing engine to generate A4 table tents. Click "Print All QR" to generate a print-ready document.
- **Print Templates**: Choose from several pre-designed templates, such as *Classic* (white card with dashed border), *Premium* (dark background with elegant styling), or *Minimal*.

## Live Table View

The **Live View** tab provides a real-time, bird's-eye view of your restaurant's status. It acts as a digital floor plan.

Tables are represented as color-coded cards:
- **Gray (Empty)**: The table has no active session.
- **Amber (Occupied)**: Customers are currently at the table and have an open session.
- **Green (Paid)**: The table has paid their bill and is ready to be cleared.

Clicking on any table card opens a detailed modal showing the current session's orders, the names of the seated customers, and the payment status.
