# Kitchen Display System (KDS)

*(Available on the Enterprise plan)*

The **Kitchen Display System (KDS)** replaces traditional paper tickets with a real-time, digital Kanban board designed specifically for cooks, chefs, and kitchen expeditors. It tracks prep times, flags delayed orders, and keeps kitchen staff and waitstaff perfectly coordinated.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **High-Contrast Kitchen Interface**: Designed with a glare-resistant dark theme, bold typography, and large touch targets optimized for wall-mounted touchscreens or rugged kitchen tablets.
- **Kanban Flow**: Tickets move seamlessly across four stages: **Awaiting Payment**, **New**, **In Progress**, and **Ready**.
- **Live Elapsed Timers**: Every ticket features a real-time elapsed timer. Tickets pending for more than 15 minutes are automatically highlighted with an urgent red warning.
- **Detailed Ticket Specifications**: Clearly displays dish quantities, customer preparation choices (doneness, sizes), paid add-ons, and customer allergy notes in bold red.
- **24-Hour Ticket History**: A toggleable history panel allows chefs to review or recall completed orders from the past 24 hours.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Kitchen Staff**: Can log in using their personal 4-digit PIN on kitchen tablets and are taken directly to the KDS interface (`/staff/kitchen`).
- **Owners and Managers**: Can open the KDS anytime by clicking the **Kitchen** button in the dashboard header.

---

## The KDS Columns

1. **Awaiting Payment** *(Violet Header)*: Displays orders placed by guests who selected digital payment at table. Once payment is confirmed, the ticket moves automatically to New.
2. **New** *(Blue Header)*: Incoming orders that need to be started. A notification chime alerts the kitchen when a new ticket lands in this column.
3. **In Progress** *(Amber Header)*: Dishes currently being cooked, plated, or prepared at the bar.
4. **Ready** *(Emerald Header)*: Dishes that are plated and waiting at the pass for waitstaff pickup and delivery to the table.

---

## How Kitchen Staff Use the KDS

1. Open the kitchen tablet and enter your 4-digit **Kitchen PIN** on the keypad.
2. The full-screen KDS board opens displaying all active kitchen tickets.
3. When a new ticket arrives in the **New** column:
   - Review the dishes, preparation options, and highlighted customer notes.
   - Tap the ticket card once to advance it to **In Progress**.
4. When cooking is complete and the food is plated at the pass:
   - Tap the ticket card in the **In Progress** column to move it to **Ready**. Waitstaff dashboards and POS screens immediately reflect that the table's food is ready for delivery.
5. When the runner takes the food to the table:
   - Tap the ticket in the **Ready** column. The ticket is marked as completed and moved to the 24-hour history archive.

---

## How to Review Past Tickets (History Panel)

If an expeditor needs to check an earlier ticket or resolve a question about an order served earlier in the shift:

1. Click the **History** button (clock icon) at the top right of the KDS screen.
2. A sliding panel opens displaying all completed tickets from the past 24 hours.
3. Review ticket items, timestamps, and staff attribution.
4. Click the close icon to return to the active kitchen board.

---

## Important Notes

- **Device Setup**: The KDS runs inside any modern web browser on a tablet, touchscreen all-in-one computer, or smart TV mounted in the kitchen.
- **Instant Status Sync**: When a cook taps a ticket to Ready, the table card in the manager dashboard and the waiter's POS terminal update immediately, letting waitstaff know the dish is ready for pickup.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Accidentally Advanced a Ticket**: If you tap a ticket by mistake, open the **History** panel to review its details or notify the floor staff.
- **Screen Sleeps During Shift**: On your kitchen tablet or computer, adjust the device's operating system settings to disable automatic screen timeout/sleep while plugged into power.
