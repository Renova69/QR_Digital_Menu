# Service Points (Non-Table Ordering)

While the platform is primarily built around table-specific QR codes, not all venues operate with traditional table service. For these scenarios, the platform supports **Service Points**.

## What are Service Points?
Service Points are distinct physical locations where customers can scan a QR code and order, without being tied to a specific seating table. Examples include:
- A bar counter
- A takeaway pickup window
- Poolside cabanas
- Hotel room service

## Managing Service Points
Service points are managed in the same Dashboard tab as regular tables, but they are logically separated. 
- You can generate and print unique QR codes for each service point.
- When a customer orders from a Service Point, the ordering flow remains exactly the same on their device.
- For staff, orders arriving from a Service Point are clearly labeled (e.g., "Bar Counter" instead of "Table 4"), ensuring there is no confusion about where the order originated or where it should be delivered.
