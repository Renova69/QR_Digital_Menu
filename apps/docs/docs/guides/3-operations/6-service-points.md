# Service Points (Non-Table Ordering)

*(Available on Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans)*

Not all venue orders originate from traditional dining tables with chairs and numbers. Renova supports **Service Points** for locations such as bar counters, takeaway pickup windows, poolside lounges, patio standing areas, and room service.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Flexible Location Ordering**: Place QR codes at standing bar counters, pickup windows, or outdoor loungers where traditional table numbering does not apply.
- **Clear Order Routing**: When guests order from a Service Point, the ticket in your dashboard, Waiter POS, and Kitchen Display System is clearly marked with the location's name (e.g., *"Bar Counter"* or *"Takeaway Window"*) instead of a table number.
- **Independent QR Generation**: Download high-resolution branded QR codes for counter stands, window decals, or acrylic displays.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners and Managers**: On Starter, Professional, and Enterprise subscription tiers.

---

## How to Create a Service Point

1. In the dashboard sidebar, click **Tables**.
2. Select the **Service Points** sub-tab (next to Live View and Tables & QR).
3. Click the **Add Service Point** button.
4. Enter the details:
   - **Service Point Name**: E.g., "Main Bar Counter", "Pickup Window", "Cabana 4".
   - **Type**: Choose the operational category (such as Counter, Takeaway, or Pickup).
5. Click **Create Service Point**.
6. The service point is created and appears in your list with its unique QR code.

---

## Downloading and Printing Service Point QR Codes

1. Under **Tables > Service Points**, locate the service point in the list.
2. Click the **QR Code** button on that row.
3. The modal displays your branded QR code with your restaurant's logo.
4. Click **Download PNG** to save the file for printing on counter plaques, table-top stands, or takeaway window stickers.

---

## The Guest Experience at a Service Point

1. The guest scans the Service Point QR code at the bar counter or pickup window.
2. The digital menu opens displaying the service point header (e.g., *"Ordering at: Main Bar Counter"*).
3. The guest selects dishes or drinks, reviews the cart, and submits the order.
4. In the restaurant dashboard and kitchen display, the ticket arrives with the bold label **Main Bar Counter**, allowing staff to prepare the order for counter pickup rather than running it to a seated table.

---

## Managing Existing Service Points

- **Rename a Service Point**: Click the edit pencil icon, update the label, and click **Save**.
- **Delete a Service Point**: Click the trash icon and confirm the prompt.
- **Service Point Status**: Active orders placed at service points appear in the dashboard **Orders** view and can be filtered alongside table orders.

---

## Important Notes

- **Separate from Dining Tables**: Service points are managed independently from seated tables and are not included in the standard A4 dining table tent bulk-print sheets.
- **Payment at Counter**: If your venue uses pay-at-table, guests at service points can pay immediately from their phone, or pay cash at the register when collecting their drinks.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Staff Confused About Order Destination**: Make sure your service point names are specific (e.g., "East Bar - Stool 3" rather than just "Bar").
- **Guest Scanned Old QR Code**: If you rename or replace a service point, download and print the latest QR code to ensure seamless guest routing.
