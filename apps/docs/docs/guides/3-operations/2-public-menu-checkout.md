# Guest Experience & Digital Checkout

Renova provides a lightning-fast, phone-first dining experience. Guests scan a table QR code and interact with your menu instantly in their mobile browser—without downloading an app, creating an account, or waiting for a physical paper menu.

---

## What Guests Can Do

- **Fast Browsing**: Scroll through categorized dishes, view photos, and check prices in EUR.
- **Instant Search & Allergen Filtering**: Filter dishes by dietary preferences (Vegan, Vegetarian) or exclude specific allergens (Gluten, Dairy, Peanuts, Seafood).
- **Customized Orders**: Select sizes, doneness, and paid add-ons, or enter custom kitchen instructions (such as "Dressing on the side").
- **Table Assistance**: Tap a single button to call a waiter or request a physical cash payment at the table.
- **Pay at Table**: Review the itemized bill, choose a tip amount, split the bill, and pay securely from their phone.

---

## The Complete Guest Workflow

### 1. Scanning the Table QR Code
1. The guest opens the default camera app on their smartphone.
2. They point the camera at the table QR tent or sticker.
3. A notification banner appears with the venue's web link.
4. Tapping the link opens the digital menu, pre-assigned to their exact table number.

### 2. Navigating the Menu
- **Category Navigation**: Guests swipe through horizontal category pills (e.g., Starters, Pasta, Grill, Drinks, Desserts) to jump directly to any section.
- **Search Bar**: Guests can type a dish name or ingredient to find matching items instantly.
- **Dietary & Allergen Filter**: Tapping the filter icon opens a panel where guests can check preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Gluten-Free). Dishes that do not match are hidden from view.
- **Photo Inspection**: Tapping on any dish photo opens a full-screen image view.

### 3. Customizing an Item & Adding to Cart
1. The guest taps on a dish card.
2. If the dish has options, a customization sheet slides up:
   - **Required Variations**: The guest selects their choice (such as "Medium Rare" or "Large 500ml").
   - **Optional Add-Ons**: The guest can check optional extras (such as "Extra Cheese" or "Avocado").
   - **Special Requests**: The guest can type a short note for the kitchen.
3. The guest taps **Add to Order**.
4. If the restaurant has configured a **Perfect Pairing**, a recommendation prompt displays: *"Would you like to pair this with...?"* The guest can add the suggestion with one tap or dismiss it.

### 4. Reviewing the Cart & Submitting Orders
1. The guest taps the floating **Cart** button at the bottom of the screen.
2. If no drinks were added, the cart displays a friendly drink reminder prompting the guest to add a beverage before placing the order.
3. If the guest has a loyalty account, they can sign in with one tap to view their points balance, apply active VIP bonuses, or redeem points for discounts.
4. The guest taps **Place Order**.
5. The order is submitted directly to the restaurant's live dashboard and kitchen display.
6. A live status screen appears showing the order's progress: **Order Placed** → **In Kitchen** → **Served**.

### 5. Requesting the Bill & Paying at Table
*(When digital payments are enabled on the Professional or Enterprise tier)*

1. When finished dining, the guest taps **Request Bill** on their screen.
2. An itemized receipt displays every dish ordered at the table during the session.
3. **Bill Splitting**: Guests can choose to pay the entire bill, split the cost evenly among dining companions, or select specific items to pay individually.
4. **Tip Selection**: The guest taps a suggested tip percentage (e.g., 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%) or enters a custom tip.
5. **Payment Method**: The guest chooses their digital payment method (such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card entry) and confirms the transaction.
6. Upon payment confirmation, the table card in the restaurant dashboard turns green to notify waitstaff that the table is settled.

---

## Important Notes

- **Browser Storage**: If a guest accidentally closes their browser tab, reopening the menu from their browser history or re-scanning the table QR code immediately restores their active cart and order status.
- **Strict Server Validation**: All dish prices, option modifiers, and currency conversions are validated securely by the server during checkout to ensure total price accuracy.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Guest Accidental Order**: If a guest submits an incorrect item, they can tap **Call Waiter** or inform the staff member immediately. Staff can cancel or edit the ticket from the dashboard or POS before the kitchen begins cooking.
- **Page Does Not Load**: Check that the guest's mobile device has an active Wi-Fi or mobile cellular connection.
