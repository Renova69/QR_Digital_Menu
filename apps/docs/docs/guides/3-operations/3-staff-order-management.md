# Staff Order Management

The **Orders** view in your dashboard is the real-time command center where front-of-house staff and managers track, acknowledge, and manage incoming orders from tables and service points.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Instant Real-Time Stream**: Orders placed by guests or staff appear immediately without manual page refreshes.
- **Audio Chime Alerts**: A distinct audio notification sounds whenever a new order arrives so staff never miss a ticket during busy periods.
- **Clear Status Progression**: Move orders from **New** to **In Progress** to **Served**, keeping the entire dining room, kitchen, and guest in sync.
- **Staff & Source Attribution**: Every order displays whether it originated from a guest's QR code or a staff member's POS terminal, including the staff member's name.
- **Highlighted Special Instructions**: Customer notes (such as allergy requests or preparation preferences) are highlighted in red text to draw immediate attention.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners, Managers, and Staff**: Available on Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans.

---

## The Order Status Workflow

Every order moves through four clear stages:

1. **NEW**: Orders just placed by guests. The order card appears with a blue accent. Staff should acknowledge the order and click **Accept / In Progress**.
2. **IN PROGRESS**: The order is actively being prepared by the kitchen or bar. The order card appears with an amber accent.
3. **SERVED**: The food or beverages have been delivered to the table. Staff click **Mark as Served** to complete the ticket.
4. **CANCELED**: Orders that were voided due to guest change of mind or unavailable ingredients.

Updating an order's status automatically updates the progress bar on the customer's smartphone screen in real time.

---

## Anatomy of an Order Card

Each ticket in the Orders view contains all necessary details:

- **Table / Location Badge**: Prominently displays the table number (e.g., "Table 8") or service point (e.g., "Bar Counter").
- **Order Number & Timestamp**: E.g., `#1042` with an elapsed timer showing how many minutes have passed since the order was submitted.
- **Order Source**: A badge indicating whether the ticket was submitted via **QR Order** or entered tableside by staff (**POS**).
- **Staff Attribution**: If entered by waitstaff, displays the employee's name.
- **Items & Options**: Detailed list of dishes, quantities, chosen variations (e.g., "Size: Large"), and extras (e.g., "Extra Cheese").
- **Special Requests**: Any custom instructions typed by the guest are highlighted in prominent red text.
- **Order Total**: Summarized in both primary and secondary currencies (e.g., EUR and BGN).

---

## How to Manage Orders During a Shift

1. In the dashboard sidebar, click **Orders**.
2. Use the status filter tabs at the top to filter by **New**, **In Progress**, **Served**, or **All**.
3. When a new order arrives:
   - Review the table number and dish requests.
   - Click **Accept** to advance the order to In Progress.
4. When the kitchen notifies you that the dish is ready:
   - Deliver the items to the table.
   - Click **Mark as Served** on the order card.

---

## How to Cancel an Order

1. On the order card, click the three-dots menu or the **Cancel Order** button.
2. Confirm the cancellation prompt.
3. The order is moved to the **Canceled** tab, and the table's active balance is adjusted accordingly.

---

## Important Notes

- **Initial Audio Activation**: Modern web browsers require you to click on the screen once before they allow websites to play audio alerts. At the start of every shift, click anywhere on the dashboard screen to ensure order chimes will ring.
- **Multi-Device Synchronization**: If a manager marks an order as Served on a laptop, the order automatically updates on the kitchen tablet and the waiter's POS terminal within seconds.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Audio Alerts Not Sounding**: Ensure your device's physical volume is unmuted, check that your browser tab has permission to play sound, and click once on the Orders screen to activate browser audio.
- **Order Placed at the Wrong Table**: If a guest selected the wrong table, staff can cancel the incorrect order and re-enter it at the correct table using the Waiter POS.
