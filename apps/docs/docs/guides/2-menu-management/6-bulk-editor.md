# Menu Bulk Editor

When you have a large menu and need to adjust prices or make changes across dozens of items (for example, a seasonal price increase), editing items one-by-one can take hours. 

The **Menu Bulk Editor** solves this by giving you a powerful, spreadsheet-like interface right inside your dashboard.

## How it works

1. Navigate to **Menu > Bulk Edit**.
2. You will see your entire menu laid out in a grid, exactly like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
3. You can click into any cell (like Price, Name, or Description) and type to change it immediately.
4. **Copy and Paste**: You can even copy a column of prices from a real Excel spreadsheet and paste them directly into the Bulk Editor grid!

## Bulk Price Adjustments

If you need to increase all your prices by a certain amount (e.g., inflation adjustment):
1. Select the items you want to change (or "Select All").
2. Click **Adjust Prices**.
3. Choose either a **Percentage** (e.g., increase by 5%) or a **Fixed Amount** (e.g., increase by €1.50).
4. The system calculates the new prices and highlights them for your review.
5. Click **Save** to apply the new prices instantly to your live menu.
