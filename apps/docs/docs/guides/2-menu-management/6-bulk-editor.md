# Menu Bulk Editor

When you have a large catalog and need to adjust prices across dozens of dishes or update descriptions for a new season, editing items one-by-one is tedious. The **Menu Bulk Editor** gives you an interactive, spreadsheet-like grid right inside your browser to make rapid updates and bulk price adjustments.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **In-Grid Editing**: Click directly into any cell—dish name, price, description, category, or stock availability—and type changes instantly.
- **Bulk Price Adjustments**: Select multiple items (or your entire menu) and increase or decrease prices by a percentage (e.g., +5%) or a fixed currency amount (e.g., +€1.00).
- **Category Filtering**: Focus on one menu section at a time (such as Beverages or Desserts) or review your entire menu at once.
- **Visual Change Tracking**: Edited cells are highlighted so you can review every modification before saving.
- **Single-Click Save**: Publish all changes to your live digital menu in a single action, or discard edits if you make a mistake.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners and Managers**: Available across all subscription tiers.

---

## How to Edit Menu Details in the Grid

1. In the top navigation bar, click **Edit Menu**.
2. In the tab bar, select **Bulk Edit** (next to Items and Import/Export).
3. *(Optional)* Use the category dropdown at the top to filter items by a specific category.
4. Click on any cell you want to modify:
   - **Name**: Edit dish titles.
   - **Price**: Enter a new numeric price.
   - **Description**: Refine ingredient details.
   - **Category**: Reassign a dish to another category.
   - **In Stock**: Toggle whether the dish is currently available.
5. As you make edits, modified cells will be highlighted in blue to indicate unsaved changes.
6. Once you are satisfied with your updates, click **Save Changes** in the toolbar.
7. Your changes are immediately published to your live digital menu and POS terminals.

---

## How to Apply Bulk Price Adjustments

If you need to update prices across an entire category or your entire menu (for example, applying a seasonal price increase):

1. In the Bulk Editor table, select the checkboxes next to the items you want to update. To select everything, check the box in the table header.
2. Click the **Adjust Prices** button in the toolbar.
3. In the modal that opens, choose your adjustment method:
   - **Percentage**: Enter a value like `+5` to increase selected prices by 5%, or `-10` to apply a 10% discount.
   - **Fixed Amount**: Enter a value like `+1.00` to add €1.00 to every selected dish.
4. Review the preview values calculated by the tool.
5. Click **Apply Changes**. The updated prices will fill the table cells with changes highlighted.
6. Click **Save Changes** in the toolbar to apply the new prices to your live menu.

---

## Managing Your Table View

- **Customize Columns**: Click **Columns** in the toolbar to toggle optional fields (such as descriptions or dietary tags) on or off to keep your workspace clean.
- **Discard Unsaved Changes**: If you make an error, click **Discard** before saving to restore your original menu values.

---

## Important Notes

- **Number Formatting**: When typing prices manually, enter plain numbers (e.g., `14.50`). Do not enter currency symbols.
- **Zero-Priced Items**: Renova's Menu Health Audit will flag any items saved with a price of €0.00 unless the item is an optional free add-on.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Changes Revert on Refresh**: Ensure you clicked **Save Changes** before closing or refreshing the tab. Unsaved edits remain local until confirmed.
- **Price Adjustment Applied to the Wrong Items**: If you have not clicked **Save Changes** yet, simply click **Discard** or refresh the page to restore your previous prices.
