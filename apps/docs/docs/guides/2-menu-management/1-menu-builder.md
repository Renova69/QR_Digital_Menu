# Menu Builder

The Menu Builder is the core of your digital offering. It provides a drag-and-drop interface to construct your menu hierarchy, manage item availability, and define custom options.

## Categories & Items

Your menu is structured into **Categories** (e.g., Starters, Mains, Desserts) and **Items** (e.g., Caesar Salad, Ribeye Steak).

### Managing Categories
- **Drag & Drop**: Reorder categories by simply clicking and dragging them into your preferred sequence.
- **Images**: You can upload a banner image for each category to enhance the visual appeal of the public menu. Images are automatically optimized and resized to save bandwidth.
- **Drink Upsells**: You can mark a category as a "Drink Category". This enables the platform's automatic upselling engine to suggest drinks at checkout if the customer hasn't ordered any.

### Managing Items
- **Details**: Set the name, description, and price (supports dual-currency display).
- **Dietary & Allergens**: Tag items with dietary labels (e.g., Vegan, Spicy) and allergen warnings (e.g., Contains Nuts, Gluten). Customers can use these tags to filter the menu.
- **Availability**: Easily toggle an item as "Out of Stock" to instantly hide it from the public menu while keeping it saved in your dashboard.
- **Perfect Pairings**: You can select "Related Items" for any dish. When a customer adds the primary item to their cart, the system will prompt them with these related items as "Chef's Recommendations".

## Menu Options & Variations

Some items require customer input, such as choosing a size, cooking doneness, or optional add-ons. 

- **Variations**: Mutually exclusive choices (e.g., Size: Small, Medium, Large). The customer *must* choose exactly one.
- **Add-ons**: Optional extras (e.g., Extra Cheese, Bacon). The customer can select zero or multiple.

When creating options, you define a list of choices and a price modifier (e.g., +€2.00). The system uses strict server-side validation to ensure that customers cannot manipulate prices during checkout.

## Menu Health Audit

To ensure your menu looks professional and converts well, the dashboard includes a **Menu Health Audit** widget. This automated tool scans your menu and reports:
- **Errors**: E.g., Items with a €0.00 price, or empty categories.
- **Warnings**: E.g., Missing translations or descriptions.
- **Info**: E.g., Missing images (which can increase sales by up to 30%).

Clicking "Fix" on any audit issue takes you directly to the relevant item or category in the menu editor.
