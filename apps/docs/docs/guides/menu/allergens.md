# Allergen Information

Renova can display the 14 major EU allergen categories on menu items, including gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, and molluscs.

## Add Allergens to an Item

1. Edit the menu item.
2. Select every allergen that applies.
3. Save and verify the public-menu display.

:::caution
Renova displays the information your restaurant enters. Confirm ingredients and cross-contamination risks with the kitchen and follow the rules that apply in your jurisdiction.
:::
