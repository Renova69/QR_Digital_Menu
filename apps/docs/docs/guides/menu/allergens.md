# Allergen Information

Displaying allergens on your digital menu helps guests make safe choices and keeps you legally compliant in most jurisdictions.

## Supported Allergens

Renova supports all 14 major EU allergens:

- Gluten, Crustaceans, Eggs, Fish, Peanuts, Soybeans, Milk/Dairy
- Nuts, Celery, Mustard, Sesame, Sulphites, Lupin, Molluscs

## Adding Allergens to an Item

1. Edit a menu item.
2. Scroll to the **Allergens** section.
3. Toggle on each allergen that applies.
4. Save.

Allergen icons will appear on the item card in the guest-facing menu.

:::caution
Always verify allergen information with your kitchen. Renova displays what you enter — it is your responsibility to ensure accuracy.
:::
