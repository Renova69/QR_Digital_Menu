# Customer Accounts & Dining History

The **Customer Profile Portal** at `/profile` is the self-service hub where dining guests can view their past meal receipts, track reward points across all visited restaurants, manage active device sessions, and exercise their data privacy rights.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Itemized Dining History**: View a complete chronological record of past dine-in orders, table numbers, timestamps, ordered dishes, and itemized receipts.
- **Multi-Venue Loyalty Overview**: Track current points balances, active VIP tier multipliers (Bronze, Silver, Gold), and countdown timers for upcoming point expirations across every Renova-powered venue visited.
- **Account Linking & Security**: Link an email address with a Google account so points and orders stay unified regardless of how the diner signs in.
- **Active Session Management**: Review all smartphones and tablets currently logged into the customer account and terminate unfamiliar sessions with a single click.
- **GDPR Privacy & Data Controls**: Request an immediate downloadable archive of all personal data or submit an account deletion and anonymization request.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Dining Guests**: Any guest who creates an account using their email address (via 6-digit verification code) or **Sign in with Google**.
- **Restaurant Owners**: Benefit from higher customer retention, repeat orders, and effortless compliance with data privacy regulations.

---

## Accessing the Customer Portal

1. On the public digital menu, tap the **Account / Profile** icon in the top corner (or navigate directly to `/profile`).
2. If prompted, sign in with your email address or Google account.
3. The customer portal opens with three dedicated tabs: **Dining History**, **Loyalty & Rewards**, and **Privacy & Security**.

---

## Exploring the Customer Portal

### 1. Order History & Receipts
- Review past restaurant visits ordered chronologically.
- Tap any order to expand the full itemized check, including dishes, customized options, order total in dual currencies (EUR / BGN), and whether the order was placed via QR menu or tableside POS.

### 2. Loyalty Accounts & Expiry Countdowns
- View your total points balance for each restaurant you patronize.
- Check your current VIP Tier (e.g., *Silver 1.2x* or *Gold 1.5x*).
- **Expiring Points Tracker**: If a venue enforces a points expiration policy (e.g., 90 days), an alert displays the exact date and number of points expiring next so you can redeem them before they lapse.

### 3. Account Linking & Device Sessions
- **Link Accounts**: Connect a Google profile to an email login to prevent accidental duplicate accounts.
- **Manage Sessions**: See the device type, browser, approximate location, and last active time of all signed-in devices. Click **Log Out Other Sessions** to immediately revoke access on shared or lost devices.

### 4. Data Privacy & GDPR Controls
- **Download Personal Data**: Click **Export My Data** to receive a structured digital archive containing your profile details, dining history, and loyalty transactions.
- **Delete Account**: Click **Request Account Deletion** to erase your personal credentials and anonymize past order records in compliance with privacy laws.

---

## Important Notes

- **Passwordless Simplicity**: Diners never need to remember or reset passwords. Sign-in is handled securely via 6-digit one-time email codes or Google authentication.
- **Cross-Venue Privacy**: Restaurants can only view points and orders relating to their own venue. A diner's activity at other restaurants remains strictly private.
