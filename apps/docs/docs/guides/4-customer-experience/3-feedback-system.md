# Customer Feedback & Review Routing

Collecting guest feedback is vital for kitchen and service quality, but protecting your public reputation is just as crucial. Renova features an intelligent post-order feedback system designed to maximize positive public reviews on Google while keeping constructive criticism private for your management team.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Interactive Post-Order Prompt**: After guests complete their meal or digital payment, an engaging feedback prompt appears with animated emojis and star ratings.
- **Smart Review Routing**:
  - **4 or 5 Stars (Delighted Guests)**: Renova encourages guests to share their positive experience publicly by presenting a direct button: **Review us on Google**. Tapping it opens your restaurant's official Google Business review page.
  - **1, 2, or 3 Stars (Unsatisfied Guests)**: The interface switches to a private feedback form asking: *"What could we have done better?"* Submissions go directly to your private dashboard without prompting the guest to post publicly, allowing you to resolve issues internally.
- **Feedback Analytics**: Track your average rating, review trends, customer comments, and the total number of happy diners redirected to Google.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners and Managers**: Can review private feedback in the dashboard and configure the Google Business review link.
- **Guests**: Complete feedback on their phones after submitting orders or settling bills.

---

## How to Set Up Your Google Review Link

To ensure happy guests land directly on your Google review page:

1. Obtain your official Google review short link from your Google Business Profile (e.g., `https://g.page/r/your-restaurant/review`).
2. In your Renova dashboard, go to **Settings > General**.
3. Locate the **Google Review Link** field.
4. Paste your review URL.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## The Guest Experience

1. After placing an order or completing digital payment at the table, the confirmation screen invites the guest to rate their meal: *"How was your experience today?"*
2. The guest taps a star or emoji (from 1 to 5).
3. **If 4 or 5 Stars**:
   - A celebratory message appears: *"Thank you! We're thrilled you enjoyed your visit."*
   - A prominent **Review Us on Google** button appears.
   - Tapping it opens Google Maps directly to the review submission dialog.
4. **If 1, 2, or 3 Stars**:
   - An empathetic message appears: *"We're sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. What could we improve?"*
   - A text area allows the guest to describe what went wrong (such as slow service, cold food, or an order mistake).
   - Tapping **Send Feedback** submits their note directly and privately to your management team.

---

## How to Review Feedback in Your Dashboard

1. In the dashboard sidebar, click **Analytics**.
2. Scroll to the **Customer Feedback** section (or select the Feedback tab).
3. Key metrics include:
   - **Average Rating**: Your overall customer satisfaction score.
   - **Rating Distribution**: Visual chart showing the proportion of 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5-star ratings.
   - **Positive Feedback Rate**: The percentage of guests rating their experience 4 or 5 stars.
   - **Google Review Redirects**: The total number of guests who clicked through to leave a public Google review.
   - **Private Feedback Log**: A chronological list of private guest notes, complete with table numbers, timestamps, and order details so you can follow up with your kitchen or floor staff.

---

## Important Notes

- **Verified Diners Only**: Feedback prompts are only shown to guests who have actually submitted an active order at your venue, preventing fake or spam review submissions.
- **Google Account Handling**: When guests tap the Google review button, the review is submitted through their personal Google account on Google Maps; Renova never accesses or stores their Google credentials.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Google Button Does Not Open the Review Form**: Test your link by pasting it into a private browser tab. Ensure the URL leads directly to your Google Business Profile review dialog.
- **Guest Feedback Missing Table Information**: If a guest provided feedback on an order placed at a counter service point, the service point title will be recorded in place of a table number.
