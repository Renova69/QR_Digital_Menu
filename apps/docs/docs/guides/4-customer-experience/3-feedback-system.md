# Customer Feedback System

Gathering feedback is crucial for improving your service, but managing online reputation is equally important. The platform includes a smart post-order feedback loop designed to maximize positive public reviews while keeping negative feedback private.

## The Feedback Loop

After a customer completes an order, they are prompted to leave a star rating and a comment about their experience. The form uses interactive, animated emojis (from 😞 to 😍) to make the process engaging.

### Smart Routing
The system intelligently routes the customer based on the star rating they provide:

- **4 or 5 Stars (Positive)**: The system recognizes the customer had a great experience. It thanks them and displays a prominent button urging them to "Leave a Google Review". Clicking this opens your restaurant's exact Google Review page in a new tab. This is a highly effective way to build social proof and improve local SEO.
- **1, 2, or 3 Stars (Negative)**: The system recognizes a sub-optimal experience. The placeholder text dynamically changes to ask, "What could we improve?". When the customer submits the feedback, it is saved securely to your private dashboard. They are *not* prompted to go to Google. This allows you to perform damage control and fix operational issues without suffering public reputational damage.

## Dashboard Analytics

In your Dashboard's **Analytics** tab, you can view a comprehensive summary of all feedback received.
- **Average Rating**: Your overall score.
- **Distribution**: A breakdown of how many 1-star, 2-star, etc., ratings you received.
- **Positive Rate**: The percentage of ratings that were 4 or 5 stars.
- **Google Redirects**: A counter showing exactly how many happy customers were sent to your Google Business page.
