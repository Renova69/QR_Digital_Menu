# Custom Branding & Theming

*(Available on Professional and Enterprise plans)*

Your digital menu should feel like a natural extension of your restaurant's physical decor and brand identity. Renova's visual branding engine allows you to upload your logo, select custom typography, design a tailored 4-color palette, and test accessibility contrast in real time.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Logo Integration**: Upload your high-resolution logo. Renova displays it prominently in your digital menu header and embeds it neatly inside the center of your table QR codes.
- **Custom Typography**: Select from 15 curated fonts (including serif, sans-serif, and modern display typefaces), with independent controls for headings and body text.
- **Tailored 4-Color Palette**: Customize your menu's **Background**, **Text**, **Card Background**, and **Accent** colors (used for buttons, price highlights, and active tabs).
- **Built-In Contrast & Legibility Checker**: Renova automatically measures the color contrast between your text and background colors and alerts you if the combination would be difficult for guests to read in dim dining room lighting.
- **Light & Dark Mode Defaults**: Choose whether your menu defaults to a crisp Light theme or an ambient Dark theme when guests first scan your QR code.
- **Real-Time Live Preview**: Watch a live mobile phone preview update dynamically as you experiment with colors and fonts before saving.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners and Managers**: On Professional and Enterprise subscription tiers can customize branding under **Settings > Branding**.

---

## How to Customize Your Menu Branding

1. In your dashboard sidebar, click **Settings**.
2. Select the **Branding** tab.
3. **Upload Your Logo**:
   - In the **Logo** section, click the upload box to select an image from your computer (JPEG, PNG, or WebP format).
   - Your logo will immediately appear in the preview phone mockup.
4. **Choose Your Fonts**:
   - **Heading Font**: Choose the typeface for category titles and dish names (e.g., *Playfair Display* for fine dining, or *Inter* for modern casual).
   - **Body Font**: Choose a clean, legible font for descriptions and prices.
5. **Set Your Brand Colors**:
   - **Background Color**: The main canvas color of your digital menu.
   - **Text Color**: The primary color for titles and descriptions.
   - **Card Color**: The container background behind individual dish rows.
   - **Accent Color**: The signature color for the cart button, order buttons, and active category pills.
   - *Tip: You can use the visual color picker or paste exact Hex color codes (e.g., `#6E56F8`) provided by your graphic designer.*
6. **Check Accessibility**:
   - Look at the contrast rating below your color selections. If you see an orange warning (e.g., *"Low Contrast"*), adjust your text color slightly darker or lighter to ensure guests can read the menu easily in any lighting.
7. **Select Default Mode**:
   - Choose whether new guests see **Light** or **Dark** mode by default.
8. Click **Save Changes**. Your live digital menu and generated QR codes update immediately.

---

## Important Notes

- **Guest Theme Preference**: While you set the venue's default appearance, guests always have a subtle sun/moon toggle in the top bar to switch to their personal preference if desired.
- **QR Code Branding**: Updating your brand accent color and logo automatically updates the styling of newly downloaded or printed QR codes.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Contrast Warning Appears**: If the contrast indicator warns that text may be difficult to read, ensure there is strong contrast between your **Text Color** and **Background Color** (for example, avoid light gray text on a white background).
- **Colors Not Visible on Mobile Phone**: After clicking **Save Changes**, pull down on your smartphone screen to refresh the menu page and clear any cached styling.
