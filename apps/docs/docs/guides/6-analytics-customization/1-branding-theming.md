# Branding & Theming

Your digital menu is an extension of your restaurant's brand. The platform provides a rich, real-time theming engine to ensure the digital experience matches your physical venue perfectly.

## Customizing Your Theme

In the **Settings > Branding** tab, you have full control over the visual identity of your menu.

### Logo Upload
You can upload your restaurant's logo. The system automatically optimizes it (converting it to a fast-loading WebP format) and places it prominently at the top of your public menu and in the center of your generated QR codes.

### Typography
Select from 15 curated Google Fonts, organized into Serif, Sans-Serif, and Display categories. You can set distinct fonts for your Headings and Body text. The platform dynamically loads only the required fonts to keep the menu blazing fast.

### Color Palette
The platform features a 4-color scheme editor:
- **Background Color**
- **Text Color**
- **Card Color**
- **Accent Color** (used for primary buttons, active states, and highlights)

## Real-Time Live Preview & Accessibility
As you tweak your colors and fonts, a **Live Preview** card instantly reflects your changes, allowing you to fine-tune your design without needing to save and check the public menu repeatedly.

Furthermore, the color editor includes an automatic **WCAG Contrast Checker**. It calculates the luminance of your text against your background and warns you if the contrast ratio falls below accessible standards (e.g., if you choose light gray text on a white background), ensuring your menu remains legible for all customers.

## Dark Mode Support
The entire platform is built with dark mode in mind. You can set your venue's `defaultTheme` (Light or Dark). When a customer scans your menu for the first time, it will default to your preference, though the customer always retains a toggle to switch to their personal preference.
