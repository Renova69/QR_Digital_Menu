# Browser Push Notifications

*(Available on Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans)*

During a busy shift, staff and managers cannot always keep their eyes glued to the dashboard. Renova supports native browser push notifications that alert your team when a new order arrives or when a guest presses the call button—even if your web browser is minimized, running in the background, or your tablet screen is locked.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Background System Alerts**: Receive native pop-up notifications on your desktop, tablet, or smartphone screen whenever an event occurs.
- **Urgent Assistance Pushes**: Instant alerts when a guest requests assistance or calls for a waiter.
- **Cash Bill Pushes**: Immediate notifications when a guest requests to pay their bill with physical cash at the table.
- **Smart Click Navigation**: Clicking or tapping on a notification automatically brings Renova to the front and opens the exact order or table that needs attention.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners, Managers, and Staff**: Using a modern web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge, Firefox) on a desktop computer, tablet, or mobile phone.

---

## How to Enable Notifications

1. Open your Renova dashboard on your device.
2. An on-screen prompt will appear asking to enable notifications: *"Stay updated on new orders and table calls"*.
3. Click **Allow Notifications**.
4. When your web browser displays its permission popup (*"Renova wants to show notifications"*), click **Allow**.
5. Your device is now registered to receive alerts.

---

## What Triggers a Notification

- **New Order Placed**: E.g., *"Table 4 placed a new order (#1052) — 2 items"*. Clicking opens the **Orders** tab with the new ticket highlighted.
- **Standard Waiter Call**: E.g., *"Table 8 requested assistance"*. Clicking opens the **Assistance** tab.
- **Urgent Service Request**: E.g., *"URGENT: Table 12 needs immediate assistance"*. Displayed with a red alert priority.
- **Cash Payment Requested**: E.g., *"Table 6 requested to pay cash — €42.50"*. Clicking opens the cash settlement drawer so a waiter can bring change to the table.

---

## How to Manage or Re-Enable Notification Permissions

If you accidentally clicked "Block" or stopped receiving alerts:

1. Look at the left side of your browser's address bar (next to the website URL).
2. Click the **Padlock** or **Site Settings** icon.
3. Locate **Notifications** in the permissions list.
4. Change the dropdown setting from *Block* to **Allow**.
5. Refresh the dashboard tab.

---

## Important Notes

- **Device Do Not Disturb**: If your tablet or computer has "Do Not Disturb", "Focus Mode", or "Battery Saver" turned on, your operating system may silence notification banners until that mode is turned off.
- **Individual Device Settings**: Notification permissions are granted on a per-device basis. Enable notifications individually on every manager laptop, host stand tablet, and bar terminal that needs alerts.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Notifications Stopped Arriving**: Ensure the Renova dashboard tab is still open in your browser (it can be in the background, but the tab should not be completely closed).
- **Sound Not Playing with Banner**: Check your computer or tablet's system volume settings to ensure notification sounds are enabled.
