# Bill Splitting

*(Available on Professional and Enterprise plans)*

When a group dines together, settling the check can easily become the most awkward and time-consuming part of the meal. Renova provides a flexible **Split Bill** feature that allows diners to pay their way—either dividing the total check equally or paying item-by-item—from their own phones or through the Waiter POS.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Two Flexible Splitting Modes**:
  - **Split Evenly**: Divide the total bill into equal portions (e.g., split 2, 3, 4, or more ways).
  - **Pay Per Item**: Diners select only the specific dishes, sides, and drinks they ordered and pay their individual total.
- **Real-Time Item Draw-Down**: As soon as a guest pays for an item, that item is marked as paid for everyone else at the table, preventing double-payments or confusion.
- **Independent Tipping**: Each paying guest can add their own individual tip percentage.
- **Seamless Waitstaff Settlement**: Waitstaff using the Waiter POS can execute split payments across multiple cards or cash without doing mental arithmetic.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Dining Guests**: Can split the bill directly on their smartphones when pay-at-table is enabled.
- **Waitstaff**: Can split checks on handheld tablets using the Waiter POS.

---

## How Guests Split the Bill on Their Smartphone

1. When finished eating, the guest taps **Request Bill** on the digital menu.
2. Under payment options, tap **Split Bill**.
3. Choose the preferred split method:

### Method A: Split Evenly
1. Select the number of people sharing the cost (e.g., *"Split 4 Ways"*).
2. Renova calculates each person's exact share.
3. The guest selects an individual tip percentage.
4. The guest pays their portion using Apple Pay, Google Pay, or credit card.
5. The table's remaining balance decreases automatically for the remaining guests.

### Method B: Pay Per Item (Itemized Split)
1. The guest is presented with a checklist of every dish and beverage ordered at the table during the session.
2. The guest checks the items they personally ordered (e.g., *"1 Greek Salad, 1 Sparkling Water"*).
3. Renova calculates their specific subtotal.
4. The guest adds an optional tip and confirms payment.
5. The selected dishes are immediately greyed out and marked **Paid** on all other table phones.

---

## How Waitstaff Split the Bill on the Waiter POS

1. Open the table in the **Waiter POS**.
2. Tap the **Split Bill** button in the order drawer.
3. Select **Split Evenly** or **Per-Item Split**:
   - If **Split Evenly**, select the number of payments and swipe the first guest's card on your terminal.
   - If **Per-Item**, tap the dishes the first guest is paying for, then tap **Collect Payment**.
4. Repeat for each guest at the table.
5. Once all items or portions have been settled, Renova automatically closes the table session.

---

## Important Notes

- **Simultaneous Payment Protection**: If two guests have the bill open on their phones at the exact same moment, Renova's real-time synchronization ensures an item cannot be paid for twice. If Guest A pays for the bottle of wine, it is immediately removed from Guest B's checklist.
- **Shared Items**: Items marked as "Shared" in the POS can be split evenly or allocated to a single guest's check.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Guest Accidentally Paid for a Companion's Dish**: If a diner pays for the wrong dish, the remaining balance can be settled by the other party selecting the remaining items, or staff can resolve the discrepancy in the dashboard under **Payments**.
- **Partial Payments Remaining at Departure**: If a party leaves before all split portions are settled, waitstaff can view the outstanding balance on the table card and either collect the remainder or close the table via **Force Close**.
