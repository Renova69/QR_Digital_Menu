# Split Bill

When a large group is ready to leave, figuring out who pays for what can be a headache for both the customers and your staff. The **Split Bill** feature solves this instantly.

## How It Works

When a customer taps **"Request Bill"** on their phone, they are given two options:
- **Pay Full Amount**: Pay for the entire table.
- **Split Bill**: Divide the cost among the group.

## Splitting Options

If they choose to split the bill, they have incredibly flexible ways to do it:

### 1. Split Evenly
Perfect for groups of friends splitting a large shared meal. The customer simply selects how many people are paying (e.g., "Split 4 ways"). The system perfectly divides the total, including tips, into equal portions.

### 2. Pay Per Item
Perfect for business lunches or groups where everyone wants to pay strictly for what they ordered.
- The customer sees a checklist of every item ordered at the table.
- They select only the items they personally consumed (e.g., "1 Burger, 1 Cola").
- The system calculates exactly what they owe and they pay just that portion.
- Once they pay, those items are marked as "Paid" for everyone else at the table, preventing double-charging.

## Staff View
Your staff doesn't have to do any math. On the Waiter POS and the Dashboard, staff can see exactly which items have been paid for and which items are still outstanding, allowing them to close out the table seamlessly.
