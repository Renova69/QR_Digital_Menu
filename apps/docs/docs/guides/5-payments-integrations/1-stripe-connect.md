# Digital Payments & Reconciliation

*(Available on Professional and Enterprise plans)*

Renova allows your guests to pay their dining checks directly from their smartphones, eliminating the wait for physical bill delivery, card terminals, and paper receipts. Payments are deposited directly into your business bank account.

---

## What This Feature Does

- **Seamless Pay-at-Table**: Guests review an itemized breakdown of their orders, select a tip, and complete payment from their mobile browser.
- **Support for Popular Payment Gateways**: Connect established providers including **Stripe**, **BORICA**, **ePay.bg**, or **MyPOS** depending on your venue's banking preferences.
- **Configurable Tipping Suggestions**: Present guests with pre-set tip percentages (e.g., 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%) or custom tip amounts during checkout.
- **Real-Time Staff Alerts**: Successful payments immediately trigger an audio notification and turn the table green on the live dashboard.
- **Payment Reconciliation Queue**: Manage and resolve cash transactions, card terminal settlements, and manual table closures in one place to ensure daily accounting balances.

---

## Who Can Use It

- **Owners**: Can connect payment gateways, configure tip options, and review financial balances.
- **Managers and Waitstaff**: Can monitor paid tables, accept cash requests, and resolve reconciliation items.

---

## How to Connect Your Payment Provider

1. In your dashboard sidebar, click **Settings** and open the **Payments** tab.
2. Toggle the switch to **Enable Digital Payments**.
3. Select your preferred payment gateway:
   - **Stripe**: Click **Connect with Stripe** and complete the standard account verification form. This unlocks instant support for credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
   - **BORICA**: Configure your merchant credentials for local Bulgarian card processing.
   - **ePay.bg**: Enter your merchant client number and secret key to accept ePay payments.
   - **MyPOS**: Link your MyPOS merchant account to unify card terminal and digital payments.
4. Once completed, your gateway status will display as **Connected**.
5. Configure your **Suggested Tip Percentages** (e.g., check 10%, 15%, and 20%).
6. Click **Save Changes**. Digital payments are now active across all tables.

---

## The Pay-at-Table Experience for Guests

1. When finished dining, the guest taps **Request Bill** on the digital menu.
2. An itemized check displays all dishes and drinks ordered during the session.
3. The guest can choose to pay the full amount or split the bill. *(See [Bill Splitting](/guides/payments-integrations/split-bill))*
4. The guest selects a tip percentage or enters a custom tip.
5. The guest confirms payment via their chosen method (such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card details).
6. Once approved, the guest sees a digital receipt confirmation.
7. In the restaurant dashboard and POS, the table immediately switches to **Paid (Green)**, accompanied by an audio alert.

---

## The Payment Reconciliation Queue

Sometimes dining bills are paid outside of the digital menu (for example, when a guest pays the waiter in physical cash or on a standalone countertop terminal). The **Payment Reconciliation Queue** ensures your sales records remain balanced:

1. In your dashboard sidebar, click **Payments**.
2. If any table was closed manually or if a transaction requires staff confirmation, it appears in the **Reconciliation Queue**.
3. Staff can select an action:
   - **Resolve**: Confirm how the bill was settled (e.g., "Paid Cash at Table" or "Settled via Physical Terminal").
   - **Reopen Session**: If the table was closed by mistake while guests are still dining, reopen the session.
   - **Dismiss**: Clear erroneous test warnings.
4. All resolved transactions are reflected in your daily end-of-shift sales reports.

---

## Important Notes

- **Direct Payouts**: Renova does not hold your restaurant's funds. All customer payments flow directly through your connected payment gateway into your designated bank account according to your provider's payout schedule.
- **Currency**: Digital payments and guest receipts use EUR.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Customer Card Declined**: The guest's phone displays an on-screen notice inviting them to try another payment method or tap **Call Waiter** to pay with cash.
- **Table Remains Amber After Guest Claims Payment**: Check the **Payments** tab to verify that the transaction was successfully authorized. If the payment did not complete, staff can provide a physical card terminal or collect cash.
