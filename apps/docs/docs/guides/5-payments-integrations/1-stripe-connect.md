# Payment Providers & Reconciliation

The platform integrates directly with multiple payment gateways to allow customers to pay their bills straight from their phones, making table turnover faster and taking pressure off your staff.

## Supported Payment Gateways

We support several major payment providers so you can choose the one that works best for your business:

1. **Stripe Connect**: The most popular option for international digital payments. Customers can pay quickly using Apple Pay, Google Pay, or entering their credit card details.
2. **BORICA (EMV-3DS)**: The standard Bulgarian national payment gateway, ensuring strict security and local compliance.
3. **ePay.bg**: A widely used payment gateway in Bulgaria, supporting both card payments and direct micro-account transfers.
4. **MyPOS**: Ideal for businesses that already use MyPOS physical card terminals and want to unify their digital payments.

## Enabling Payments

To turn on digital payments:
1. Go to **Settings > Payments** in your dashboard.
2. Choose your preferred provider from the list.
3. Follow the secure connection steps for your chosen provider (for example, clicking "Connect with Stripe").
4. Once completed, your account status will show as "Connected" and your customers can begin paying digitally!

## The Pay-at-Table Experience

When a customer is ready to leave, they simply tap **"Request Bill"** on their digital menu on their phone. They can review their items, leave an optional tip, and pay securely using their preferred card. 

### Staff Notifications
When a payment is successful, your staff instantly hear a notification chime, and the table on the Live Dashboard turns Green (Paid) so staff know it's time to clear the table.

## Payment Reconciliation Queue

Sometimes, payments happen outside of the digital menu (for example, a customer pays in cash or uses a physical card terminal). 

The **Payment Reconciliation Queue** is a special tool for your staff to manage these offline payments and ensure all your accounting adds up perfectly.

If a digital payment is interrupted or if a table is closed out manually by a waiter, the system will flag the transaction in the Reconciliation Queue. 
- **Resolve**: Staff can mark the payment as completed (e.g., "Customer paid in cash instead").
- **Reopen**: If a mistake was made, staff can reopen the table session.
- **Dismiss**: Ignore the warning if it was a test or an error.

This ensures your end-of-day financial reports are always 100% accurate.
