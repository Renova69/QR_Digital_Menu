# Getting Started with Renova

Welcome to Renova — the all-in-one digital menu and restaurant management platform.

## What you can do with Renova

- **Digital QR Menu** — Create a beautiful, mobile-friendly menu your guests access by scanning a QR code.
- **Online Ordering** — Accept orders directly from your menu with integrated checkout and payment.
- **Table Bookings** — Let guests reserve tables online.
- **POS System** — Manage orders from a dedicated point-of-sale interface.
- **Kitchen Display** — Show incoming orders to your kitchen staff in real time.
- **Customer Feedback** — Collect ratings and reviews from guests after their visit.

## Quick Start

1. **Register your restaurant** — Create your account and complete the onboarding wizard.
2. **Build your menu** — Add categories, items, images, and prices.
3. **Generate your QR code** — Print and place it on your tables.
4. **Go live** — Guests can now browse and order from their phones!
