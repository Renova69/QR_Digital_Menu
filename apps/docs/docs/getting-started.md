# Getting Started with Renova

Renova combines a guest-facing digital menu with optional ordering, payments, reservations, staff, POS, kitchen, and feedback workflows.

## Quick Start

1. Create your account and complete restaurant onboarding.
2. Add menu categories and items, including prices and availability.
3. Add tables or other service points in **Tables & QR Codes**.
4. Download or print a QR code and scan it as a guest.
5. Test the complete guest flow before placing QR codes in your venue.

## Enable Features Gradually

Start with the menu and QR codes. Enable ordering, payment providers, reservations, staff devices, printing, and loyalty only when your venue is ready to operate each workflow. Availability depends on your subscription and restaurant configuration.

Use **Back to App** in the navigation bar to return to Renova.
