# Getting Started with Renova

Welcome to Renova! Renova is an all-in-one digital menu, table ordering, and restaurant management platform. It allows your guests to scan QR codes with their smartphones, browse your dishes with allergen and dietary tags, place orders, call waitstaff, and pay their bills—without downloading any app.

At the same time, Renova gives your team a central dashboard to manage incoming orders, track live table statuses, run a Kitchen Display System (KDS), take tableside orders on mobile POS devices, accept table reservations, and build repeat business with loyalty rewards.

## Quick Start Guide

Follow these five steps to launch your digital menu in minutes:

### 1. Create Your Account & Complete Onboarding
When you sign up at the registration page:
1. Enter your email and password, then confirm the 6-digit verification code sent to your email. You can also click **Sign in with Google**.
2. Select your subscription tier (Free, Starter, Professional, or Enterprise) and choose monthly or annual billing.
3. Enter your **Restaurant Name**, your full name, and your initial number of tables.
4. If you chose a paid tier, complete the checkout step to activate your subscription instantly.
5. Click **Go to Dashboard** to enter your workspace.

### 2. Set Up Your Restaurant Profile
1. In the left sidebar, click **Settings**, then open the **General** tab.
2. Confirm your **Restaurant Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Address**. This information appears in the footer of your public menu.
3. Verify your **Timezone** (e.g., `Europe/Sofia`). Setting your correct timezone ensures that scheduled menus, happy hours, and sales metrics calculate accurately.
4. Set your **Dashboard Language** and choose any **Target Languages** for automatic menu translation.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

### 3. Build Your Digital Menu
1. Click **Edit Menu** in the top navigation bar to open the Menu Editor.
2. Under the **Items** tab, click **Add Category** (e.g., "Starters", "Mains", "Drinks").
3. Click on a category in the left column, then click **Add Item**.
4. Enter the item's name, description, price, dietary tags (e.g., Vegan, Spicy), and allergens (e.g., Gluten, Dairy).
5. Upload a clear, appealing photo of the dish.
6. Click **Save**.
7. If the dish has variations (such as Size or Doneness) or add-ons (such as Extra Cheese), click **Manage Options** to configure them.

### 4. Create Tables & Generate QR Codes
1. In the left sidebar, click **Tables**.
2. Open the **Tables & QR** sub-tab and click **Add Table**.
3. Enter a table number or name (e.g., "Table 1", "Terrace 4") and select a Zone (e.g., "Main Hall").
4. Click **Create Table**. Renova instantly generates a unique QR code.
5. Click **Printable QR Codes** to choose a table tent template (Classic, Premium, or Minimal) and print table cards for your venue.

### 5. Test the Guest Flow
1. Open the camera on your smartphone and scan one of your printed table QR codes.
2. The digital menu opens in your mobile browser.
3. Browse the categories, add an item to your cart, and review the checkout screen.
4. Place a test order. In your dashboard under **Orders**, you will hear a notification sound and see the new order card appear in real time.

## Dashboard Navigation

The Renova dashboard is organized into clear workspaces:

### Desktop Header
- **Restaurant Switcher**: If you operate multiple venues, switch between them using the dropdown at the top left.
- **Edit Menu**: Opens the menu builder, import/export tools, and bulk spreadsheet editor.
- **POS**: Opens the tableside Point of Sale for staff (Enterprise plan).
- **Kitchen**: Opens the full-screen Kitchen Display System (Enterprise plan).
- **View Menu**: Opens your live customer menu in a new browser tab.
- **Language & Theme Toggles**: Switch dashboard language or toggle between light and dark display modes.

### Sidebar Navigation
- **Summary**: Your operational bird's-eye view showing menu view metrics, daily revenue, active tables, top dishes, and recent orders.
- **Orders**: Real-time incoming order stream with status buttons (New, In Progress, Served) and audio notifications.
- **Assistance**: Table service calls and cash bill requests from seated guests.
- **Tables**: Live floor plan with status colors (Empty, Occupied, Paid), table setup, service points, and QR code printing.
- **Payments**: Transaction history and the Payment Reconciliation Queue for offline payments.
- **Reservations**: Booking calendar, availability controls, and seating zone capacity.
- **Analytics**: Comprehensive sales performance, popular items, peak hour graphs, and Excel exports.
- **Settings**: Venue configuration, loyalty program, payment gateways, staff access, custom branding, subscription management, and thermal printers.

## Common User-Facing Issues

### 1. "My menu edits do not appear when I scan the QR code"
- **Cause**: The category or item might be marked as Hidden or Out of Stock, or your mobile browser may be showing an older cached view.
- **Solution**: Open the Menu Editor, verify that the category availability is set to **Always Available** (or currently within its scheduled hours), and ensure the item's **Available** toggle is active. On your phone, pull down to refresh the page.

### 2. "I cannot hear audio alerts when new orders arrive"
- **Cause**: Web browsers require an initial user interaction before playing sound on a page.
- **Solution**: Click anywhere inside the **Orders** tab when opening your dashboard at the start of a shift. Ensure your computer or tablet volume is turned up and that browser tab permissions allow sound.

### 3. "The QR code opens the menu but does not show a table number"
- **Cause**: The QR code may have been saved from a generic link rather than the specific table card.
- **Solution**: Navigate to **Tables**, locate the specific table row, click the QR code icon, and download or print the code directly from that table card.

### 4. "A waiter or cook cannot log in with their PIN"
- **Cause**: The PIN was entered incorrectly, the staff member is assigned an administrative role that requires email login, or the tablet has not been enrolled.
- **Solution**: Verify under **Settings > Staff** that the employee has the `Waiter` or `Kitchen` role. Owners and Managers must log in using their email and password. On the staff tablet, scan the venue's device enrollment code if prompted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do my customers need to download an application from the app store?
No. Renova runs completely inside standard mobile web browsers. Guests simply scan the QR code using their phone camera and the menu opens immediately.

### Can I run Renova with just a digital menu before taking orders?
Yes. The Free plan gives you complete digital menu editing, allergen tags, QR code generation, and scan tracking. You can switch on online ordering whenever your team is ready.

### How do I change my subscription or update billing details?
Go to **Settings > Subscription** and click **Manage Billing (Stripe Portal)**. From there, you can upgrade your plan, switch between monthly and annual billing, download invoices, or update your credit card.

### Can I use Renova across multiple dining rooms or terraces?
Yes. You can organize your tables into custom **Zones** (such as Main Dining, Terrace, Patio, Bar). In the Waiter POS and Live Table View, staff can filter tables by zone to quickly locate guests.
