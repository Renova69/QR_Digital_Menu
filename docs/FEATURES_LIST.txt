1. 🍽️ Customer-Facing (Public Menu & Ordering)
Branded Digital Menu: A responsive, mobile-first web menu loaded via table QR codes (/menu/public/:restaurantId?table=:name). Injects custom brand colors, typography, and default themes from the restaurant profile.
Smart Filter & Search: Full-text menu search, dietary filters (Vegan, Spicy, New, etc.), and allergen exclusion pills.
Pinch-to-Zoom Lightbox: Integrated gesture-driven image viewer for high-res menu items.
Variation & Add-on Modifiers: Supports complex choices (e.g., sizing, doneness, extra toppings) with dynamic price modifiers.
Multi-Currency Pricing: Displays prices in both EUR and BGN concurrently using the Bulgarian National Bank (BNB) fixed conversion rate.
Upselling Engine ("Perfect Pairings"): Deterministic upsells at item-level (pairings) and checkout-level (suggesting drinks if none are present in the cart).
Call Waiter & Assistance: Standard or urgent assistance requests sent to staff with a call cooldown system.
Customer Feedback Loop: Star-rating and comments collection; ratings ≥4 redirect users directly to Google Reviews.
2. 💳 SaaS Subscription Tiers & Stripe Payments
4-Tier SaaS Model: Features are dynamically locked or unlocked via 
feature.service.ts
 across four subscription plans: FREE, STARTER, PROFESSIONAL, and ENTERPRISE.
Stripe Connect Integration: Connects restaurant Stripe accounts to collect payments directly from the table, charging a platform fee on each transaction.
Dynamic Feature Guarding: Frontend and backend endpoints are guarded by plan checks utilizing 
useFeature.ts
.
3. 🎁 Loyalty & Retention Engine
FIFO Point Ledger: Advanced point management system defined in 
loyalty.service.ts
. Points are handled as discrete batches with expiry dates to prevent concurrent database transactional lockups.
Timezone-Aware Happy Hour: Integrates timezone-aware happy hour windows with multipliers on points earned.
VIP Tiers: Automates progress tracking (Bronze, Silver, Gold) with spend multipliers.
Daily Expiry Reminders: Midnight UTC cron job alerts users of points expiring soon.
4. 🧑‍🍳 Staff POS & Kitchen Display System (KDS)
Waiter POS Layout: A dedicated interface (/staff/pos) that uses an ephemeral, in-memory shopping cart enabling waiters to take orders directly at tables.
Kitchen Display System (KDS): Full-screen live view (/staff/kitchen) showing real-time active order cards, statuses, and special requests.
Real-time Synchronization: Utilizes WebSocket push notifications via 
events.gateway.ts
 to push new orders, status changes, and waiter call alerts instantly.
Device Enrollment & PIN Login: Scanned QR codes enroll devices to staff-only PIN login screens, requiring a 4-digit PIN instead of a password.
5. 🛠️ Restaurant Operations (Owner Dashboard)
Drag-and-Drop Menu Builder: Visually sort menu categories and items via @dnd-kit/sortable with real-time database updates.
Lazy Menu Translations: Connects to the DeepL API to automatically translate category names, descriptions, and choices on-demand (and caches translations to PostgreSQL JSON columns).
Menu Health Audit: Scans menus for issues like zero-priced items, missing descriptions, or lack of pictures, offering direct quick-fix navigations.
Live Table Management: Real-time grid dashboard showing tables as colored cards (empty, occupied, or paid).
Bulk QR Code Exporter: Print-ready A4 templates and PNG downloads for table QR codes.
Analytics XLSX Exporter: Downloads platform sales and metrics into spreadsheets.
6. 👑 Platform Super-Admin Panel
Operational Dashboard: Located at /super-admin, platform administrators can view aggregated system metrics, suspend/reactivate tenants, perform soft deletes/restores, and override active subscription tiers with automatic local cache invalidation.
